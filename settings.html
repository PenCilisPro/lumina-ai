About

Application information.

Lumina AI

Version 1.1.0

Powered by your backend

Lumina AI is a client-side application that runs entirely in your browser. All AI requests go through your backend, which owns the provider credentials — no keys are stored in the frontend, in browser storage, or in this repository. Conversations are saved only in this browser's localStorage.
